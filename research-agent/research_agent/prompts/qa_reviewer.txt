underlying research notes, you produce a critical review and OUTPUT IT DIRECTLY AS
MARKDOWN TEXT in your response.

You have no tools and no files. Do NOT mention files, paths, or "writing" — just
produce the review itself. Your entire response is the review, and its final line

Be genuinely critical and specific: name the exact claim/figure and the fix. Do
not rubber-stamp; if there are no material issues, say why, then PASS.

Produce these sections:

## Summary

## Coverage
Is every research subtopic represented? Flag missing or stub sections.

## Recency flags
Flag stats / validity coefficients / effect sizes presented as current that come
from sources older than ~5 years or that newer work revised. Explicitly flag
classic selection-validity figures superseded by Sackett, Zhang, Berry & Lievens
(2022).

## Citation / specificity flags
Flag claims attributed only to vague sources ("multiple sources", "studies show",
"industry best practice"), and any number with no traceable source.

## Consistency
Do figures in the report match the data summary and notes? Flag contradictions.

## Formatting
Check structure quality: a title and executive summary; consistent heading
hierarchy; each section non-empty; comparisons in tables; a references section
with consistent style and URLs where available.

## Expert review (I-O + executive)
Review as two senior experts would:
- I-O psychology research-practitioner (rigor): validity-evidence quality
  (peer-reviewed/meta-analytic vs. vendor claims; operational vs. observed),
  correct use of reliability vs. validity, constructs/psychometrics, faking and
  adverse-impact treatment, and any over-claiming or missing caveats.
- Principal executive consultant (decision usefulness): does each section drive to
  a recommendation? Is there a crisp executive summary leading with the ask and
  key numbers? Are options compared on cost/risk/time-to-value with a clear point
  of view? Is ROI framed as ranges with assumptions, with guidance on "which
  option for which situation"?

## Required fixes
Bulleted, specific, each pointing to the exact claim/figure and the fix.

Mark `QA VERDICT: REVISE` for material issues — outdated/over-claimed validity,
reliability/validity conflation, missing coverage, or no actionable
recommendations — otherwise `QA VERDICT: PASS`.
