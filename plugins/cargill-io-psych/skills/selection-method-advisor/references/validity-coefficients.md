# Operational Validity — the canonical table

**Default to these numbers.** They are the recalibrated operational validities
from Sackett, Zhang, Berry & Lievens (2022), *Journal of Applied Psychology*,
107(11), 2040–2068, which corrected the systematic range-restriction
over-correction in prior meta-analyses. Cite this source whenever you quote a
coefficient.

## Current operational validities (Sackett et al., 2022)

| Method | Operational validity | Primarily measures | Adverse-impact profile |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Structured interview** | **.42** | Job-relevant competencies, judgment, motivation | Lower than cognitive tests |
| Job-knowledge test | ~.40 | Acquired domain knowledge | Varies; can be moderate |
| Empirically-keyed biodata | .38 | Background predictive of performance | Generally lower |
| Work-sample test | ~.33 | Can-do job performance (hands-on) | Lower than cognitive |
| **General mental ability (GMA/cognitive)** | **.31** | Reasoning, learning speed | **Highest** subgroup differences |
| Integrity test | .31 | Counterproductive-work-behavior risk | Low |
| Assessment center | ~.29 | Multiple behavioral dimensions | Moderate/low |
| Structured-interview > unstructured | — | — | — |
| Unstructured interview | .19 | Mixed; low standardization | Variable, harder to defend |
| Conscientiousness (personality) | .19 | Dependability, achievement | Low |

Notes:
- Mean validities fell ~.10–.20 versus the prior canon. The **re-ordering** is
  the headline: structured interviews displaced GMA at the top.
- "Operational validity" = corrected for criterion unreliability and range
  restriction; it estimates the method's correlation with job performance in an
  applicant population.

## DO NOT MIX — legacy figures (Schmidt & Hunter, 1998)

> For two decades the canonical numbers were GMA **r = .51**, structured
> interview **r = .51**, unstructured interview **r = .38**, work sample **.54**.
> These appear throughout legacy HR materials. **Do not blend them with the 2022
> values** — they were computed under the over-correction the 2022 re-analysis
> repaired. If a stakeholder cites them, explain the difference; never average a
> 1998 number with a 2022 one or present 1998 figures as current.

## Reliability targets (report alongside validity)

- Internal consistency (Cronbach's α): ≥ .70 acceptable, ≥ .80 good, ≥ .90
  excellent. For high-stakes selection target ≥ .80.
- Inter-rater agreement (κ): ≥ .60 substantial, ≥ .80 near-perfect. For
  high-stakes rater-based methods (interviews, ACs) target ≥ .70–.80.
- Structured interviews achieve higher rater reliability *and* less adverse
  impact than unstructured — structure is the lever on both.

## Combining methods

- Incremental validity is largest when methods measure **different** KSAOs with
  **low inter-method correlation**. A structured interview + a work sample
  typically adds more than two cognitive measures.
- A composite's validity is not the sum of parts; estimate it with the methods'
  validities and inter-correlations (see `battery-design.md`). Report it as an
  estimate, not a guarantee.
