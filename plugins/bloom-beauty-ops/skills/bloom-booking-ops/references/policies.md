# No-show, cancellation & deposit policies

Industry-grounded templates. Benchmarks reflect common salon practice (24–48h
notice; late-cancel 50%; no-show 100% or flat fee; deposit 20–50%). They are
defaults to discuss with the owner — **not legal advice**, and enforceability +
disclosure rules vary, so verify with the appropriate Minnesota authority and the
booking/payment processor before charging fees.

## Why a policy matters

No-shows and last-minute cancellations cost a lash studio real money (industry
estimates put the average at several thousand dollars a year) and waste slots a
waitlisted client would have taken. A clear, kindly-worded, consistently-applied
policy is the single biggest lever — paired with deposits and automated reminders.

## Default policy parameters

| Lever | Common default | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Cancellation notice | **24–48 hours** | 24h is most common/flexible |
| Late-cancellation fee | **50%** of service | Inside the notice window |
| No-show fee | **100%** of service (or flat $25–$50) | Strongest deterrent |
| Deposit to book | **20–50%** of service | Applied to the service; forfeited on no-show/late-cancel |
| Grace / exceptions | Owner discretion | First-time grace, emergencies, illness |

## Client-facing policy (template)

> **Booking & Cancellation Policy — Bloom Beauty Suites & Lash Bar**
> Your appointment is reserved just for you. To book, a [20–50]% deposit holds
> your spot and is applied to your service.
> Need to reschedule? Please give at least [24/48] hours' notice — just reply to
> your reminder or text [number].
> • Cancellations inside [24/48] hours: [50]% of the service.
> • No-shows: [100]% of the service (or your deposit).
> Life happens — reach out and I'll always do my best to work with you. 🤍

## Short versions

- **Booking confirmation line:** "I hold spots with a [X]% deposit and ask for
  [24/48]h notice to reschedule."
- **Bio/booking-page line:** "[X]% deposit to book • [24/48]h cancellation
  policy."

## Enforcement tactics (most effective, least friction)

1. **Deposits / prepay** at booking — the biggest no-show reducer.
2. **Automated reminders** at 48h and 24h with one-tap confirm/reschedule.
3. **An active waitlist** so a freed slot is filled fast (see
   `references/workflows.md`).
4. **Apply the policy consistently** — selective enforcement breeds resentment;
   keep a small discretion for genuine emergencies.
5. **Card on file** via the booking processor (only with clear consent and per
   the processor's rules).

## Minnesota / compliance caveat

- Charging a card for a fee requires clear, advance disclosure and the client's
  agreement at booking, plus compliance with the payment processor's rules.
- Confirm any consumer-notice or disclosure requirements with the appropriate
  Minnesota authority. This document is operational guidance, not legal advice.
- SMS reminders/marketing require opt-in consent; keep a record of consent.
