# Hiring Process — posts, interview, trial scorecard, onboarding

Real selection discipline, salon-sized. The two highest-validity, lowest-bias
signals are a **standardized structured interview** and a **work sample** (the
trial set) — this process leans on both.

## Job post template (lash tech)

> **Lash Artist wanted at Bloom Beauty Suites & Lash Bar — Maple Grove, MN 🌸**
> We're a warm, beautiful suite studio looking for a [classic/volume] lash artist
> who does retentioned, clean work and makes clients feel cared for.
> **You bring:** a valid Minnesota license to perform lash extensions, [X]+
> months of lashing, great sanitation habits, reliability, and a friendly chair-
> side manner.
> **We offer:** [pay model — see classification.md], a gorgeous private suite,
> [supplies/booking/marketing support], and a supportive owner (hi, I'm Renee).
> **To apply:** send a few photos of your work + your license info to [contact].
> A short interview + a paid trial set are part of the process.

## Structured interview (same questions, same order, every candidate)

Rate each 1–5 against the anchor (5 = strong evidence, 3 = adequate, 1 = concern).

1. **Craft & range** — "Walk me through how you'd map and apply a volume set for
   a client with sparse natural lashes." *(Looks for: isolation, weight/curl
   choice, retention logic.)*
2. **Sanitation** — "Describe your setup and clean-down between clients."
   *(Looks for: tool disinfection, single-use items, lash-bed hygiene.)*
3. **Retention/problem-solving** — "A client says her lashes fell out in 4 days.
   What do you do?" *(Looks for: diagnosis, accountability, no blame-the-client.)*
4. **Reliability** — "Tell me about your current booking rhythm and how you
   handle a day you're sick." *(Looks for: dependability, communication.)*
5. **Client care** — "How do you handle a client who's quiet or unhappy with the
   look?" *(Looks for: warmth, listening, recovery.)*
6. **Fit/logistics** — availability, license status, start date.

Keep notes on observed behavior, not impressions.

## Trial-day scorecard (the work sample) — BARS

Paid trial: the candidate completes a set (model or comp'd client). Score each
dimension 1/3/5 on observable behavior; decision rule below.

| Dimension | 5 — Strong | 3 — Adequate | 1 — Concern |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Craft / finish** | Even, mapped, clean lines; style matches the brief | Solid set, minor unevenness | Gaps, stickies, wrong curl/length |
| **Isolation & retention technique** | Perfect isolation, correct adhesive use, no stickies | Mostly isolated, occasional fix | Poor isolation, stuck lashes |
| **Sanitation** | Flawless setup, disinfection, single-use discipline | Generally clean, 1 reminder | Skips steps, contamination risk |
| **Speed** | On time for the service window | Slightly over | Far over time |
| **Client interaction** | Warm, consultative, comfortable | Pleasant, a bit quiet | Awkward or dismissive |
| **Professionalism** | Prepared, coachable, tidy station | Fine | Late, defensive, messy |

**Decision rule:** no dimension at 1; craft, isolation, and sanitation each ≥ 3
(ideally craft/retention ≥ 4). One trained observer minimum; a second opinion on
the photos reduces bias. Photograph the set for the record.

## Onboarding checklist (employee or contractor)

- [ ] Verify & copy valid **MN license**; record number + expiry
- [ ] Proof of liability insurance (esp. contractors/renters)
- [ ] Paperwork per classification (W-4/I-9 for employees; W-9 + written
      agreement for contractors) — see `classification.md`; confirm with a pro
- [ ] Tax/payment setup (payroll vs 1099; how/when paid or rent collected)
- [ ] Booking system access, calendar, deposit/cancellation policy briefing
- [ ] Suite/station setup, keys/access, supply expectations
- [ ] Brand & service standards walk-through (menu, prices, sanitation SOP)
- [ ] First-week check-in scheduled

## Renter move-in checklist

- [ ] Signed lease/agreement (have an attorney review the template)
- [ ] Deposit + first month's rent received
- [ ] License + insurance verified and on file
- [ ] Suite condition photos; keys/access; house rules acknowledged
- [ ] Added to the suites-manager app (tenant, lease terms, rent, due date)

## Renter-vetting rubric (score 1–5)

| Factor | Looks for |
|---|---|
| Valid license + insurance | Current, correct profession |
| Book stability / experience | Established clientele or credible plan |
| Payment reliability | References from prior landlord/suite |
| Professionalism & fit | Clean, reliable, good neighbor in a shared space |
| Profession mix | Complements current tenants (avoid direct over-saturation) |

Recommend a fit verdict (Strong / Conditional / Pass) with the rationale.
