# Bloom Post Templates

Reusable patterns in Bloom's voice. Adapt every time — swap real details, never
ship the bracketed skeleton. Keep it warm, personal, first-name, short-sentence.

## Caption skeletons

**Before/after (social proof)**
> Hook: "[Client first name]'s [style] set ✨" or "From bare to [adjective] 👀"
> Body: one line on the look (curl/length/style) and how it suits them.
> CTA: "Booking link in bio 🤍" / "DM 'LASH' to book your set."
> Hashtags: #maplegrovelashes #twincitieslashes #[style]lashes #lashextensions #mnlashartist
> Consent line (internal): confirm client okayed this photo.

**Educational (save-worthy)**
> Hook: "Classic vs Hybrid vs Volume — which is you?"
> Body: 2–3 crisp lines; one tip per line. End with "Not sure? I'll help you pick."
> CTA: "Book a consult + first set 🤍"

**Aftercare**
> Hook: "Want your lashes to last? 3 things 👇"
> Body: no water 24h • cleanse daily w/ lash shampoo • no oil-based products.
> CTA: "Save this for later 💌 Due for a fill? Tap to book."

**Behind-the-scenes / story**
> Hook: "A peek inside the suite at Bloom 🌸"
> Body: the calm, the playlist, the ring light — make it feel like a treat.
> CTA: "Your me-time is one tap away."

**Offer / openings (booking)**
> Hook: "A few fill spots open this week ⏳"
> Body: name the days/times; restate the [deposit/cancellation] policy in one line.
> CTA: "Grab one here → [link]"

**Rebooking nudge**
> Hook: "Lashes feeling sparse? It's fill o'clock 🕐"
> Body: gentle reminder of the 2–3 week fill rhythm.
> CTA: "Rebook in 10 seconds → [link]"

## Reel / TikTok ideas (short-form)

- "POV: you wake up with perfect lashes" morning routine.
- 15-sec aftercare tip.
- Classic → Hybrid → Volume comparison on three clients.
- "Day in the suite" time-lapse.
