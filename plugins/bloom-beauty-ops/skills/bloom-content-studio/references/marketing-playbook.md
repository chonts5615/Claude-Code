# Bloom Marketing Playbook

Practical, local-first marketing for a Maple Grove lash studio + suite-rental
business. Benchmarks are cited with general industry sources; treat them as
starting points, not guarantees.

## Posting cadence & content pillars

Default: **3–5 posts/week**, rotating these pillars so the feed isn't all sales:

| Pillar | ~Share | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Social proof | 30% | Before/after sets, client reactions, reviews, milestones |
| Educational | 25% | Lash aftercare, "classic vs hybrid vs volume," retention tips, FAQ |
| Behind-the-scenes | 20% | The suite, a fill in progress, product picks, owner story |
| Offer / booking | 15% | This week's openings, promos, gift cards, new services |
| Rebooking nudge | 10% | "Due for a fill?" prompts, loyalty perks |

Reels/TikTok outperform static for reach: lash close-ups, satisfying
application clips, transformations, quick aftercare tips. Aim for 1–3 short-form
videos/week.

## Google Business Profile (highest-ROI local lever)

For "Maple Grove lashes / lash extensions near me" visibility:
- Complete every field: services + prices, hours, attributes, booking link,
  service area, photos (add fresh photos weekly — GBP rewards recency).
- Post weekly via GBP Posts (offers, what's new, events).
- **Reviews are the ranking + conversion engine** — see below.
- Keep NAP (Name, Address, Phone) identical everywhere online (citations).

## Review generation

- Ask at checkout, right after the reveal ("If you love them, a quick Google
  review means the world"). Send a follow-up text with the direct review link
  the same day.
- Make it one tap: a short link to the Google review form.
- Respond to every review (thank positives by name; address negatives calmly,
  move specifics to DM/phone).
- Never gate reviews or offer payment for them (against platform + FTC rules).

## Referrals & retention offers

- Referral: give-and-get ("Refer a friend — you both get $15 off your next
  fill"). Track in the app's client notes.
- Win-back lapsing clients: the beauty app already flags **lapsing/lost**
  clients (see `bloom-booking-ops`); pair a warm message with a small comeback
  offer.
- Loyalty: every Nth fill perk, or a bonus for booking the next 3 fills upfront.

## Seasonal calendar (Maple Grove / MN rhythm)

| Season | Hooks |
|---|---|
| Jan–Feb | "New year, low-maintenance lashes"; Valentine's gift sets |
| Mar–May | Spring refresh; prom & grad lashes; Mother's Day gift cards |
| Jun–Aug | Wedding/bridal season (trial + day-of); vacation/waterproof-friendly looks |
| Sep–Oct | Back-to-routine; fall tones; "treat yourself" |
| Nov–Dec | Holiday parties; gift cards; book-ahead-for-NYE |

## No-show / deposit note for promo posts

When a promo drives new bookings, restate the booking policy briefly (deposit /
cancellation window) so new clients know it up front — keeps the no-show rate
down. Full policy logic lives in `bloom-booking-ops`.

## Content-calendar deliverable layout

A dated table, one row per post:

| Date | Channel | Pillar | Hook | Caption | Asset needed | CTA | Notes (consent/FTC) |

Group by week; flag posts that need a photo/video the owner must capture, and
flag any gifted/comped collab needing an FTC disclosure.

## Compliance reminders (always)

- **FTC**: disclose gifted/sponsored/influencer content clearly (#ad, "gifted").
- **Consent**: explicit client permission before posting photos or testimonials.
- **Honest claims**: describe the look; avoid medical or guaranteed-result
  language.
